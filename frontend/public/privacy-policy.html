Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

The short version. Hub Trade helps small construction and trade businesses manage jobs, crews, vehicles and equipment. We collect only what we need to run your account.

We do not sell your information. We do not rent it, trade it, or share it with third parties for their own advertising or marketing. A small number of service providers (for example, our payment processor and hosting provider) handle data for us only to operate the app, as described in Section 4.

This policy explains what information Hub Trade (the “App”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App is operated by Summit Office Solution (“we,” “us,” or “our”). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, company name and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form, not as plain text.
  • Business records you enter: jobs (including job addresses and customer names), employees (names, email, phone, job title, trade and pay rate), vehicles (including license plate), equipment, and related maintenance dates and notes.
  • Estimates and photos: estimate details, customer names and email addresses, and photos you upload.
  • Messages and support requests: messages sent inside the App and any data-entry or support requests you submit.
  • Time cards: check-in and check-out times recorded by you or your crew.

Information collected automatically

  • Activity records: a log of actions taken in your company account (for example, who created or edited a job), which owners and admins can review.
  • Usage and device information: how the App is used, such as screens viewed, and basic device and browser details. This may include session recordings of App activity that we use to find and fix problems.
  • Sign-in data: a secure token stored on your device to keep you signed in.

The App does not collect your device’s GPS or precise location.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number. We receive limited details such as your plan, payment status and billing email.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the App, including jobs, crew assignments, time cards, estimates and reminders.
  • To process payments and manage your membership.
  • To email estimates to your customers when you choose to send them, and to send account and service messages.
  • To give support and data-entry help.
  • To keep the App secure, prevent misuse, and fix problems.
  • To improve the App.
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. We do not sell your information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing or advertising, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

5. Information about your employees and customers

If you enter information about your employees or customers, you are responsible for having the right to do so. We handle that information on your behalf to provide the App to you and do not use it for any other purpose.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your information, generally within 30 days, except for records we must keep for billing, tax, security or legal reasons.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections, hashed passwords, and role-based access so people in your company see only what their role allows. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You can access, correct or export your company’s information from within the App. You can also ask us to delete your account or information at any time by contacting us below. Depending on where you live, including California, you may have additional rights to know, access, correct and delete your personal information. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

9. Children

The App is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will notify you in the App or by email.
